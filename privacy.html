Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • HAOMI Studio

1. Local-First Architecture

HAOMI Studio is designed with privacy-by-default architecture. All video rendering, keyframing, audio processing, canvas compositing, and OFTS project saves happen directly on your local device (browser IndexedDB and WebAssembly threads). We do not upload your media files, source video clips, or audio tracks to our servers.

2. Cloudflare Project Sharing

When you explicitly use the “Share Project” feature, your exported project JSON and necessary compressed thumbnail metadata are transmitted over HTTPS to our serverless Cloudflare Workers and stored in Cloudflare KV storage solely to generate a shareable URL. You retain full ownership and rights to any content you share.

3. Payment & Donation Processing

When you support HAOMI Studio through our Sairi QRIS gateway, the transaction is processed securely through Sairi API / Midtrans QRIS endpoints. We do not store credit card details or banking credentials. Transaction records, amount, and reference IDs are processed according to bank-grade encryption standards.

4. Local Storage and Cookies

HAOMI Studio utilizes browser localStorage and IndexedDB solely to maintain user preferences (such as selected UI theme, layout, audio volume, and offline cached projects). No third-party tracking cookies or advertising telemetry are embedded.

5. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository: github.com/haomi-cell/haomi-studio.
